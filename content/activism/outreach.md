# Public Outreach

We share our research with the public through:

- Public lectures and school visits
- Hands-on demonstrations and open lab days
- Media interviews and explanatory articles

## In the Media

### Galey Tzahal — *Korach & Sharki*, 12 November 2026

Guy Ron talks with the hosts of **קורח ושרקי** about our new *Nature* paper with the
Shema Lab at the Weizmann Institute: how an epigenetic liquid biopsy picks up
early-stage breast cancer from a single blood draw, what the single-molecule
measurement actually reads off each nucleosome, and what still stands between the
method and routine clinical use.

**8 minutes 30 seconds, in Hebrew.**
