## Research Profile

I am an experimental nuclear physicist with expertise in **precision nuclear measurements**, **detector instrumentation**, **radiation detection**, and **beamline design**, coupled with advanced **computational modelling and Bayesian inference**.  

In recent years, my research has expanded into a strongly cross-disciplinary program connecting **high-precision nuclear physics methodologies** with **single-cell and spatial omics** (CyTOF, scRNA-seq, scCUT&Tag). This work focuses on translating rigorous experimental design, simulation-anchored uncertainty quantification, and principled statistical inference into quantitative biological measurement and predictive modeling.

My group emphasizes *methodological closure*:  
experiment design → physics-guided simulation → Bayesian/statistical inference → independent cross-validation.  

We develop end-to-end detector and DAQ systems, from custom electronics through calibration and real-time analysis, and scalable pipelines for high-dimensional biological data integrating probabilistic inference, optimal transport, and multi-modal alignment.
